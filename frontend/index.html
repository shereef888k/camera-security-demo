About This Educational Demonstration:

Modern web browsers isolate hardware peripherals (cameras, microphones, sensors) behind the strict navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia() security boundary. A website can never silently capture or stream your camera feed without an explicit, user-initiated action.

This interactive platform demonstrates how informed consent, hardware isolation, and server-side Telegram transmission operate in compliance with modern privacy standards. No camera feeds or photos are captured without your deliberate interaction.

Topics Covered: Browser Permission Sandboxes • Hardware LED Status Verification • Ephemeral Demonstration Sessions • Secure Telegram Bot API Webhook Delivery.

🔒 Strict Privacy & Informed Consent Protocol
✓ No Silent Access: Opening this website never accesses or queries your camera hardware.
✓ Explicit Permission Only: Camera access is requested solely if you press "Enable Camera".
✓ No Live Streaming Feed: Demonstration does not broadcast or stream live video feeds.
✓ Review Before Send: You inspect the snapshot before choosing whether to submit or discard.
✓ Telegram Destination: Photos are sent strictly to the administrator's configured Telegram bot chat.
✓ Instant Teardown: All camera tracks stop immediately when you leave, cancel, or finish.
