Educational Protocol & Disclosures

This demonstration audits how your web browser guards device camera hardware. We strictly adhere to privacy-by-design and transparent consent principles:

  • ✓ Explicit Permission Only: Your camera is never queried automatically. Access is requested solely if you press the consent button below.
  • ✓ No Live Camera Preview: In compliance with privacy standards, no live video stream is displayed on your screen or broadcasted anywhere.
  • ✓ Automatic Single Snapshot: After permission is granted, the demonstration automatically captures one test photo.
  • ✓ Immediate Hardware Teardown: All camera tracks and sensor indicator lights stop immediately after the single snapshot is taken.
  • ✓ Confirmation Required Before Upload: You inspect the captured photo and must explicitly confirm before it is sent to the administrator's Telegram chat.
